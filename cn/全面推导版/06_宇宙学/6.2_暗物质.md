## 核心结论

$$\boxed{\text{暗物质} = \text{原初黑洞蒸发终点的 BEC 涡旋拓扑孤子}}$$

$$\boxed{M_{DM} = M_7 = 5.01 \times 10^{17}\ \text{GeV} \approx 10^{-9}\ \text{kg}}$$

$$\boxed{\Omega_{DM} \approx 0.26\quad(\text{零自由参数})}$$
