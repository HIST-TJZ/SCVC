    Main-sequence stellar structure equation: dP/dr = -GMρ/r², L ∝ ε_fusion

    → α and α_s lock the fusion rate → lock stellar luminosity → lock radius

    T_eq determined by SCVC water H-bond energy (0.20 eV) (latent heat of vaporization)
    → L ∝ M³·⁵, D_HZ ∝ √L ∝ M¹·⁷⁵
    → θ_☉ ∝ R/D_HZ ∝ M⁰·⁸/M¹·⁷⁵ = M⁻⁰·⁹⁵

  Stellar mass effect on θ_☉:
    M=0.5 ☉: θ=61.8''   (Red dwarf — enormous sun)
    M=0.7 ☉: θ=44.9''   (K-type star)
    M=1.0 ☉: θ=32.0''   (**Our Sun — 0.53°**)
    M=1.5 ☉: θ=21.8''   (F-type star — small sun)
    M=2.0 ☉: θ=16.6''   (A-type star)

  ▸ **θ_☉ ≈ 0.5° is locked by SCVC** — α and α_s precisely set this value through stellar structure
  ▸ Different mass stars have different θ_☉ (red dwarf 47', Sun 32', F-type 22')
