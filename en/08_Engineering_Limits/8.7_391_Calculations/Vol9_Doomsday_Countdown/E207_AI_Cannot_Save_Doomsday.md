## §3. AI's Own Death: Hardware Lifespan < Collapse Duration

### 3.1 Without Semiconductor Fabs, Chips Die

```
GPU/CPU degradation mechanisms (post-collapse, no temperature control, no cleanroom):

Electromigration:
  Current pushes metal atoms to migrate → wires thin/break
  High temperature + high current density accelerates → doomsday has no air conditioning
  Normal: 5-15 years (with cooling)
  Post-collapse: ~2-5 years

Gate oxide breakdown:
  MOSFET gate SiO₂ degrades over time
  High temperature accelerates → post-collapse has no constant temperature → faster

Solder joint fatigue:
  Chip-to-substrate solder joints → thermal expansion/contraction → cracks
  Post-collapse temperature swings large → accelerated failure

SSD/HDD storage:
  SSD: Floating gate charge leakage → ~1-10 years unpowered
  HDD: Magnetic domain decay + bearing seizure → ~3-5 years

  Trained AI model weights are stored on SSD/HDD
  → Storage failure = AI "brain death"
  → Even if power remains, cannot retrain (no data center cluster)
```

### 3.2 AI Death Timeline

```
Post-collapse:
  Year 1: AI runs on backup power, providing diagnosis and advice
  Year 2: Backup power fuel exhausted, or components begin failing
  Year 3: Storage media begin degrading, partial model weight loss
  Year 5: GPUs fail from electromigration — AI becomes scrap metal
  Year 10: All electronic devices have failed
```

## §4. Information vs Physics: SCVC's Fundamental Distinction

### 4.1 The Energy Gulf Between Two Layers

```
Landauer limit (minimum energy to erase one bit of information):
  kT ln 2 = 2.85 × 10⁻²¹ J/bit

Physical-layer bond breaking:
  C-C bond:  3.6 eV  = 5.77 × 10⁻¹⁹ J
  N≡N bond:  9.8 eV  = 1.57 × 10⁻¹⁸ J
  H-bond:    0.20 eV = 3.20 × 10⁻²⁰ J

Energy ratio of bit operation vs bond breaking:
  C-C bond / Landauer ≈ 200×
  N≡N bond / Landauer ≈ 550×
  Even the "weak" H-bond: ~11×

This is the ultimate root of AI's physical impotence:
  AI lives in a ~10⁻²¹ J/bit world.
  The operations doomsday demands live in a ~10⁻¹⁹ J/bond world.
  The gulf is approximately 200×.
  
  AI can optimize which bonds to break.
  But it cannot reduce the energy required to break them.
```

### 4.2 This Is Not a "AI Isn't Strong Enough Yet" Problem

```
No matter how powerful AI becomes:
  Landauer limit is a thermodynamic floor → cannot go lower
  Bond energies are locked by α → cannot be changed
  
  AI will never be able to break a C-C bond with 10⁻²¹ J.
  Because the bond does not care how smart the operator is.
  It only cares about energy.
  
  This is not a scaling problem → it is a category error.
  "Better AI" cannot solve problems in the wrong category.
```
