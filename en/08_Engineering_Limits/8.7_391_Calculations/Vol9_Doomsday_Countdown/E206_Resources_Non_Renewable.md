## §1. Coal: The Carboniferous Window Has Been Permanently Closed by White-Rot Fungi

### 1.1 The Carboniferous "Perfect Storm" (360–300 Mya)

Three conditions happened to coincide — remove any one and it fails:

```
Condition 1: Lignin had just evolved
  C-C bond 3.6 eV → lignin provides structural support → trees can grow to 30 m
  → Massive terrestrial biomass → enormous organic carbon

Condition 2: Lignin-degrading enzymes did not yet exist
  White-rot fungi had not yet evolved → no organism could decompose lignin
  C-C bond 3.6 eV is too stable → no enzyme active site could break it
  → Dead trees did not rot → they accumulated

Condition 3: Vast anoxic swamps
  Pangaea assembly → enormous low-lying swamps
  Dead trees sank into anoxic mud → not oxidatively decomposed
  → Burial → pressure + temperature → coal
```

This window lasted approximately 60 million years.
The coal formed during this period = all the "easy energy" used by the human Industrial Revolution.

### 1.2 Why It Will Never Happen Again

```
After the Carboniferous ended (~300 Mya):
  White-rot fungi evolved: laccase, manganese peroxidase (MnP)
  
  These enzymes finally found a catalytic pathway to break the C-C bond at 3.6 eV.
  From that moment on:
    Dead tree → colonized by fungi within weeks → fully decomposed within years → CO₂ back to atmosphere
    Forests do not become coal → they become mushrooms.

Modern net coal formation rate ≈ zero:
  Global photosynthetic carbon fixation:  ~120 GtC/year
  Respiration + decomposition return:     ~120 GtC/year
  Net burial (peat):                      ~0.1-0.2 GtC/year — this is peat, not coal

Even if we deliberately create anoxic swamps:
  Peat accumulation rate:    ~0.5-1 mm/year
  To form a 1 m thick peat layer:  1,000-2,000 years
  Compress into coal (10:1 volume ratio): 10,000-20,000 years → only 1 meter of coal
  Industrial Revolution needed >10 m thick outcrop seams: >100,000 years
```

**SCVC Verdict:**
```
  C-C bond 3.6 eV → chemical stability of lignin
  → Evolution took 60 million years to find enzymes to decompose it
  → Once those enzymes appeared → evolution does not reverse
  → The coal window has been biologically permanently closed
  
  It is not "need to wait a few million years"
  It is "never"
  Unless you reverse 300 million years of evolution — which is harder than reversing entropy
```

## §2. Oil: Modern Ocean Circulation Has Shut the Anoxic Preservation Window

### 2.1 The Demanding Conditions for Oil Formation

```
① Marine plankton bloom → massive organic matter sinking
② Bottom water anoxia → organic matter not oxidized → preserved
③ Rapid burial → sediment cover → temperature-pressure conversion → hydrocarbons
④ Reservoir + cap rock → does not escape to the surface

Jurassic-Cretaceous (200–66 Mya):
  Multiple global anoxic events (OAEs) → precisely correspond to major source rock intervals
  Global ocean circulation was slow then → deep ocean anoxic → extremely high organic preservation rate
```

### 2.2 Why It Will Never Happen Again

```
Modern oceans:
  Thermohaline circulation (AMOC etc.) → polar cold water sinks → deep water fully oxygenated
  Organic matter sinking → decomposed by mid-water bacteria en route
  Preservation rate: <0.1% of primary productivity
  Net oil formation rate: ≈ zero (several orders of magnitude slower than human extraction rate)

Global warming may cause localized anoxia:
  But scale is nowhere near Cretaceous global anoxic events
  Even if organic matter is buried → needs tens of millions of years to mature → human civilization cannot wait
  
  → Modern ocean circulation is a "permanent brake" on oil formation
  → Unless you stop global thermohaline circulation (which first means AMOC collapse = human extinction)
```

## §3. High-Grade Ores: All Are "One-Time Earth Events"

### 3.1 Iron: The Great Oxidation Event Will Not Repeat

```
Banded Iron Formations (BIFs, ~2.5 Bya):
  Oceans were rich in dissolved Fe²⁺
  Cyanobacteria produced O₂ → precipitated Fe³⁺ → iron oxide layers on the seafloor
  This was a SINGLE geological event tied to the rise of atmospheric oxygen
  The oceans today are oxygenated → dissolved Fe²⁺ is near zero
  → BIF-type iron deposits will never form again
```

### 3.2 Copper, Tin, Rare Earths: Grade Declines in One Direction

```
High-grade vein deposits:
  Formed by hydrothermal fluids concentrating metals along fractures
  Near-surface deposits accessible to pre-industrial technology → all mined out
  Remaining deposits are deeper, lower-grade, and require industrial technology to access
  → E205 Bolt ②: no industrial civ = no access to remaining ore

Grade is a one-way street:
  Humanity always mines the highest grade first (lowest energy cost)
  What remains is progressively lower grade → progressively higher energy cost
  This is the entropy tax (E205 §4)
```
