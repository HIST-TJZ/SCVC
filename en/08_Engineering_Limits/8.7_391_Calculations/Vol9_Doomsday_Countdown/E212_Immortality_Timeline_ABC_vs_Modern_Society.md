## 1. Definition of Longevity Escape Velocity

### 1.1 What Is "Escape Velocity"

```
Definition: Annual medical/biotechnological progress adds >1 year of healthy human lifespan

State              Annual Extension    Outcome
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Sub-escape          0.3 yrs/yr        You age 1 year, medicine compensates 0.3 years
                                      Net aging +0.7 yrs/yr → will eventually die

Critical            1.0 yrs/yr        You age 1 year, medicine compensates 1.0 years
                                      Net aging 0 → lifespan paused, but not extended

Escape              1.1 yrs/yr        You age 1 year, medicine compensates 1.1 years
                                      Net extension +0.1 yrs/yr → getting further from death

Strong escape       1.5 yrs/yr        Net extension +0.5 yrs/yr → rapidly distancing from death
                                      Beyond this, no need to calculate specific lifespan ceiling

"Escape velocity" is not immortality — it is "death can never catch up with you"
 
Key insight:
  Before escape: you are racing against time, and losing
  After escape:  time is racing against you, and can never win
  
  Escape velocity is not a "technology" — it is an "intersection point"
  Before this intersection, each round of breakthroughs buys "more research time"
  After this intersection, immortality is a natural consequence; no further "solving" needed
```

## 4. Immortality Coverage — The Mathematical Necessity of 100%

### 4.1 Why "Partial Immortality" Is Unstable

```
Immortality coverage model:

  Let p = fraction of population with access to immortality technology.
  Let L(p) = social stability as a function of coverage.

  L(p) = p × (1 − (1−p)^k)

  Where k = revolt threshold multiplier (~3-5 based on historical revolution dynamics)

  Stability curve:
    p = 0:     L = 0     (no one immortal → everyone accepts death → stable but terminal)
    p = 0.01:  L ≈ 0.01  (1% immortal → 99% accept momentarily)
    p = 0.05:  L ≈ 0.02  (5% immortal → resentment brewing)
    p = 0.10:  L ≈ 0.05  (10% immortal → active resistance forming)
    p = 0.30:  L ≈ 0.10  (30% immortal → revolution threshold approached)
    p = 0.50:  L ≈ 0.15  (50% immortal → unstable equilibrium, tipping point)
    p = 0.80:  L ≈ 0.40  (80% immortal → system breaking down)
    p = 0.99:  L ≈ 0.95  (99% immortal → nearly stable, but the 1% exclusion breeds violence)
    p = 1.00:  L = 1.00  (100% immortal → stable equilibrium)

  → The ONLY stable equilibrium is p = 1.00
  → p = 0 is NOT stable in a world where immortality technology is known to exist
  → Once immortality is proven possible, the excluded will revolt
  → "Knowing your neighbor will live forever while you die → the most powerful revolutionary motivation ever created"
```

```

  1. You will extend your life 30 years → 30 years of research → breakthroughs extend you 50 more → 50 more years → more breakthroughs → escape

     → Civilization's knowledge density continuously grows → breakthroughs accelerate

  5. Difference from "mind uploading"
     → Uploading: you die, a copy lives (but is that copy you?)
     → Rolling immortality: YOU live, biological continuity preserved
     → H-bond 0.20eV (consciousness continuity) → biological continuity = you remain you

  6. Ultimate: ABC's three "immortalities" converge
     → Biological immortality: rolling lifespan extension → escape velocity
     → Social immortality: Layer B permanent record → remembered after death
     → Civilizational immortality: climate managed + no ultimate owner + no war → civilization never collapses

     The three immortalities reinforce each other:
        Social immortality (reputation) → incentivizes research → biological immortality
        Biological immortality → more time for research → civilizational immortality more robust
```
