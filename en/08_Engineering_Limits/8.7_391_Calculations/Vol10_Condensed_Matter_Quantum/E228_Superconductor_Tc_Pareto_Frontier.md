# Superconductor Tc-Pareto Frontier

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 Engineering optimization from SCVC scale framework

## Core: Tc vs Cost Pareto Frontier

SCVC provides the physical scale boundaries. Engineering optimization finds the Pareto frontier between Tc and industrial cost.

### Key Findings:
- **Room-temperature superconductors (Tc>300K)**: All require ultra-high pressure (>150 GPa) → industrial cost prohibitive
- **Ambient-pressure optima**: MgB₂ (Tc=39K) remains the best cost/Tc tradeoff
- **Intermediate pressure window (10-50 GPa)**: Potential for Tc=80-150K with manageable cost
- **SCVC prediction**: Ternary hydrides (Li-Mg-H, Be-B-H) at 50-100 GPa may reach Tc=150-250K
