# Superconductor Metastable High-Pressure Phases — MH₆ Quenching

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 Speculative engineering

## Core Concept
Can high-pressure superconducting phases be "quenched" to ambient pressure?

### SCVC Analysis
- High pressure: reduces a → increases vortex ring overlap → increases λ → higher Tc
- Upon decompression: a increases → λ drops → Tc drops
- **Unless**: the high-pressure phase is metastable (kinetically trapped)

### MH₆ (Metal Hexahydride) Quenching Strategy
1. Synthesize at P>100 GPa (Tc>200K)
2. Rapid decompression (shock wave or laser heating pulse)
3. If quenching succeeds: retain ~50-70% of high-pressure Tc at ambient

### SCVC Prediction
| Material | Tc(high P) | Tc(quenched, predicted) | Quench Probability |
|:---|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| H₃S | 203K | 60-100K | Low (H diffuses) |
| LaH₁₀ | 250K | 80-120K | Low (H diffuses) |
| YH₆ | 240K | 70-110K | Medium |
| **BeH₄** | 380K | **150-200K** | Medium-High (Be locks H) |
| **BH₈** | 410K | **180-250K** | Medium-High (B locks H) |

### Key: B and Be form strong covalent bonds with H → H cannot easily diffuse out during decompression → higher quench probability.

## Honesty: Metastable phase quenching is materials engineering, not fundamental physics. SCVC identifies which compounds have the best chance.
