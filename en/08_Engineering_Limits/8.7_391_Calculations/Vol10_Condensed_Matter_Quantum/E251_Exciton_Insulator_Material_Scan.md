# Exciton Insulator Material Scan

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 SCVC screening criteria

## Core
Exciton insulator = spontaneous exciton condensation in semiconductors/semimetals with small band overlap.

### SCVC Screening Criteria
1. Small or negative band gap (E_g ≤ exciton binding energy)
2. Small effective mass (large exciton radius)
3. High dielectric constant (weak screening for strong binding)

### Key SCVC Insight
Exciton insulator = "charge conjugate" of superconductor. Same Ampère pairing mechanism, opposite charges.
→ SCVC superconductor framework directly transfers to exciton insulator search.

## Honesty: Exciton insulator experimental confirmation is challenging (order parameter is charge-neutral). SCVC provides search heuristics.
