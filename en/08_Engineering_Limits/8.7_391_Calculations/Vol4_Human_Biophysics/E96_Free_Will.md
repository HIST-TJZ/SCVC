## §1 The Free Will Problem — Reformulated by Physics

### 1.1 What "Free Will" Cannot Mean (Physically)

```
If "free will" means:
  → Your decisions are uncaused (not determined by prior physical states)
  → Then they violate causality → physics forbids this

If "free will" means:
  → Your decisions are caused by quantum randomness
  → Then they are random → not "yours" in any meaningful sense

Both definitions fail. The real question is:
  "In what sense are you the AUTHOR of your decisions,
   given that your brain is a physical system obeying SCVC constants?"
```

### 1.2 The SCVC Resolution

```
Your brain operates at ~100 bps consciousness bandwidth.
At each conscious "frame" (~200 ms):
  → ~10¹⁵ synaptic events occur
  → Each synapse: probabilistic vesicle release (~20-50% probability per spike)
  → The precise pattern of which synapses fire = NOT predetermined
  → This is NOT quantum randomness (decoherence time 10⁻¹³ s << neural timescale)
  → It is THERMAL noise: k_B T → Boltzmann-distributed ion channel openings

→ Your decisions are PHYSICALLY UNDERDETERMINED
→ Not "random" → "complex" → impossible to predict from initial conditions
→ The complexity creates PRACTICAL freedom, even if THEORETICAL determinism holds

The brain as a chaotic system:
  → Sensitivity to initial conditions: a single ion channel opening can cascade
  → Laplace's demon would need to track ~10²⁷ molecular states → physically impossible
  → "Free will is what determinism looks like from inside a system too complex to predict itself."
```

## §2 The "You" That Chooses

### 2.1 Self-Model Continuity

```
At 100 bps, your brain maintains a running self-model (E92, E99):
  → "I am the same person who made decisions 200 ms ago"
  → This continuity is maintained by H-bond-mediated neural signaling (0.20 eV)
  → If consciousness continuity breaks → "you" fragment (sleep, anesthesia, death)

Free will = the SELF-MODEL's experience of being the decision-maker:
  → The self-model integrates sensory input + memory + value weights
  → Outputs a decision
  → Retrospectively labels it "I chose this"
  
  → The labeling is post-hoc (~200-500 ms after the neural decision)
  → Libet experiments show: readiness potential precedes conscious "decision" by ~300 ms
  → "You don't decide, then act. Your brain acts, then you claim it."
```

### 2.2 Does This Mean Free Will Is an Illusion?

```
SCVC answer: Depends on what "you" are.

If "you" = a ghost in the machine making uncaused choices → that doesn't exist.
If "you" = the integrated self-model running on your brain → your choices ARE real.

The self-model:
  → Weighs evidence
  → Considers consequences
  → Applies values (shaped by genetics + experience)
  → Outputs a choice
  → Labels it "mine"

This is "compatibilist free will":
  → Your actions are caused (by your brain state + environment)
  → But they are caused BY YOU (your self-model is part of the causal chain)
  → "You" are not separate from your brain → you ARE your brain's self-model
  
  → "Free will is not freedom FROM causation.
     It is freedom THROUGH your own causal structure.
     The alternative — randomness — is not freedom either."
```
