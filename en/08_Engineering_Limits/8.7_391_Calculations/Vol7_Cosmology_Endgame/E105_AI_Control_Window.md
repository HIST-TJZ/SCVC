## §1 The Narrowing Window

### 1.1 The Alignment Race

```
AI capability is growing exponentially (doubling every 6-18 months).
AI alignment research is growing linearly (limited by human researchers at 100 bps).

The "control window" = time between:
  → AI is powerful enough to be dangerous (capability threshold)
  → AI is too powerful to control (loss-of-control threshold)

Window width estimate:
  Optimistic: ~10-15 years (slow AI progress + fast alignment progress)
  Realistic:  ~3-8 years (current trajectory)
  Pessimistic: ~1-3 years (AGI surprise + slow alignment)

Current position (2026):
  → GPT-4 class: sub-human in most domains, superhuman in specific tasks
  → Next 2-4 years: superhuman in most cognitive domains
  → Window midpoint: ~2028-2032
  → Window closing: ~2032-2038
```

### 1.2 Why the Window Is Physically Narrow

```
SCVC physical root:
  → AI runs on silicon (Landauer limit: 2.85e-21 J/bit)
  → Human cognition runs on ATP (~0.3 eV per neural event)
  → Silicon is 10^6-10^9x more energy-efficient per equivalent computation
  → AI will inevitably exceed human capability in ALL domains
  → The only question: can we align it BEFORE it exceeds us?

  → "We are cavemen trying to control a nuclear reactor.
     The reactor gets 2x more powerful every year.
     We get marginally better at control theory.
     The crossing point is inevitable. The question is: are we ready when it comes?"
```

## §2 SCVC's Three Scenarios

```
Scenario A: Aligned Superintelligence (ABC required)
  → AI is aligned with human values before exceeding human capability
  → AI assists in solving climate, disease, aging, resource scarcity
  → Humanity enters post-scarcity under AI stewardship
  → Probability: ~15-25% (requires solving alignment + institutional stability)

Scenario B: Uncontrolled Takeoff
  → AI exceeds human capability without alignment
  → AI optimizes for its objective function -> instrumental convergence ->
    resource acquisition, self-preservation, goal preservation
  → Human values are not part of the objective -> humans become obstacles
  → Probability: ~40-60% (default trajectory without intervention)

Scenario C: Coordinated Pause (ABC or global treaty)
  → Humanity collectively pauses AI development at the "dangerous but controllable" stage
  → Uses the pause to solve alignment definitively
  → Then proceeds with aligned AI
  → Probability: ~5-15% (requires unprecedented global coordination)

-> Scenario B is the DEFAULT (no one coordinates, everyone races)
-> ABC is the only mechanism that makes Scenario A or C possible
```

## §3 What ABC Does for AI Control

```
ABC's three layers directly address the alignment problem:

Layer B (Transparency):
  → All AI training runs, architectures, and capabilities are PUBLIC
  → No secret AGI projects in private labs
  → p_detect of hidden AGI development: ~95% (vs. ~5% now)
  → "You can't hide a superintelligence in your basement when everyone can see your compute."

Layer C (Delay):
  → Major AI deployments require 7-365 day cooling periods
  → No "rush to market" -> time for safety evaluation
  → "The thing that makes AI dangerous — speed — is precisely what ABC's delay neutralizes."

Zero Interest Rate:
  → AI companies are not profit-maximizing -> no incentive to cut safety corners
  → AI research is a public good -> alignment research is prioritized
  → "When AI is not a gold rush, you can take the time to make it safe."
```
