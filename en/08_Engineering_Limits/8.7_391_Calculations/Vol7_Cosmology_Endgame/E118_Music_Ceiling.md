## §1 The Mathematical Basis of Harmonics

`
When two frequencies have small-integer ratios:
  2:1 = octave (most consonant)
  3:2 = fifth
  4:3 = fourth
  5:4 = major third

Common feature: low information complexity.
  Fundamental + overtones = simple superposition → clean neural signal → "comfortable."

Dissonant intervals:
  Frequency ratios are irrational or large-integer ratios → complex superposition → neural noise → "uncomfortable."

"Comfortable" and "uncomfortable" are not cultural constructs.
They are direct results of cochlear physics + neural synchronization rate:
  Consonance → hair cells fire synchronously → clean signal → "comfortable"
  Dissonance → hair cells fire chaotically → neural noise → "uncomfortable"

Cultural differences lie in: which interval combinations are used.
Physical commonality lies in: octaves and fifths are "comfortable" in ALL cultures.
`

   The listener''s brain builds a predictive model of "the next note."

   Note matches prediction → micro dopamine reward → "comfortable" (no surprise)

4. Why "complex" music is "unpleasant" the first time but "addictive" after repeated listening:
   First time: cannot build prediction → "unpleasant" (Δ explosion)
   After multiple times: neural relearning of predictive model → prediction established → safe resolution → "addiction"
   This shares the same neural circuit as addiction: new predictive model = new reward source.
