# E144-E158: Engineering Limits — Medical and Environmental

## E144: Immune Response Speed Upper Limit
- Antigen presentation: ~6-24 h (MHC-I peptide loading + transport to surface)
- T cell clonal expansion: ~3-7 days (doubling time ~6-12 h, E168 DNA polymerase constraint)
- Antibody production: ~5-14 days (B cell → plasma cell differentiation + somatic hypermutation)
SCVC: immune response floor ~3-5 days for adaptive, ~hours for innate.

## E145: Wound Healing Maximum Rate
- Epidermal migration: ~0.1-0.2 mm/day (keratinocyte motility limit)
- Collagen deposition: ~0.5 mm/day (fibroblast synthetic capacity)
- Angiogenesis: ~0.1-0.3 mm/day (E170 endothelial cycle constraint)
SCVC: full-thickness wound closure: 1-2 mm/day maximum. Cannot exceed cell division + migration physical limits.

## E146: Antibiotic Resistance Ceiling
- Mutation rate: ~10⁻⁹ per base per generation (DNA polymerase, E168)
- Horizontal gene transfer rate: ~10⁻⁴-10⁻⁶ per cell per generation
- Resistance evolution: requires ~3-5 mutations for high-level resistance
SCVC: resistance emergence time ~10⁶-10⁹ bacterial generations → can be delayed by multi-drug combinations (requiring simultaneous resistance mutations).

## E150: Self-Healing Material Rate Upper Limit
SCVC: analogous to wound healing (E145). Polymer chain diffusion ∝ T/η (viscosity). Physical ceiling: ~μm/hour for bulk materials, ~mm/day for microcapsule-based systems.

## E152: Geothermal Energy Upper Limit
Earth heat flux: ~0.087 W/m² (radioactive decay + primordial heat). Total: ~44 TW. Harnessable: ~1-10 TW.

## E153: Atmospheric Pressure Life Limit
Maximum: ~100 atm (hyperbaric, enzyme denaturation from pressure). Minimum: ~0.1 atm (Armstrong limit, water boils at body temperature).

## E154: Display Pixel Density Upper Limit
Human eye angular resolution: ~1 arcminute → at 30 cm viewing distance → ~300 ppi sufficient, ~600 ppi indistinguishable. SCVC: photoreceptor spacing ~2-3 μm → retinal pixel density ~8000 ppi equivalent.

## E155: Glacier Maximum Flow Rate
Ice viscosity ∝ exp(-E_a/k_B T), E_a ∝ (H-bond rearrangement energy) ∝ α². Maximum: ~10 km/year (Jakobshavn Glacier).

## E156: Maximum Rainfall Rate
Atmospheric column water vapor capacity ∝ exp(L_v/R_v T). Maximum: ~100 mm/h for extreme events, ~500 mm/h theoretical ceiling (atmospheric moisture convergence limit).

## E158: Drug Resistance Ceiling — Medical Revolution
Combination therapy (≥3 drugs with non-overlapping resistance mechanisms): resistance probability = (μ)^(n_drugs) × (population size). For 3 drugs: ~10⁻²⁷ per cell → effectively zero for tumor sizes <10¹² cells. SCVC: resistance is a numbers game, solvable by combinatorial pressure.
