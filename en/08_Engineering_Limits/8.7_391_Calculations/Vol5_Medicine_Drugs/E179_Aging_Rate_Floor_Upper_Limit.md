# E179: Aging Rate Floor — Physical Lower Bound

**SCVC**: Aging = accumulation of molecular damage (DNA mutations, protein cross-links, mitochondrial damage).

### Physical Damage Rate
```
DNA damage rate ~10⁴ lesions/cell/day (mostly repaired)
Unrepaired lesions accumulate at ~1-10/cell/year
Mutation accumulation: ~10⁻⁸ per base per year (somatic mosaicism)
```

### Aging Rate Floor
```
Minimum aging rate = (unrepairable damage rate) × (functional decline per lesion)
```

SCVC: Unrepairable damage includes:
- Spontaneous deamination (C→U) ∝ k_B T/(H-bond energy) ∝ k_B T/α²
- Depurination (glycosidic bond hydrolysis) ∝ exp(-E_a/k_B T)
- Telomere shortening (E168)

### SCVC Absolute Floor
```
Maximum healthy lifespan ≈ 120-130 years (current human maximum)
This is set by the cumulative effect of multiple parallel damage processes
each with physical rate floors from molecular bond energies (∝ α²).
```

### Can We Go Beyond?
- **Damage repair enhancement**: Possible (better DNA repair enzymes, protein turnover)
- **Damage prevention**: Limited by physical chemistry (you can't stop deamination without changing α or T)
- **Periodic rejuvenation**: Most promising — clear damage faster than it accumulates

**SCVC: True biological immortality would require changing α or k_B T. Not possible in this universe.**
