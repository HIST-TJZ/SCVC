# E186-E191: Longevity — Physical Pathways and Complete Protocol

## E186: Longevity Physical Pathways
SCVC identifies three physical longevity strategies:
1. **Damage repair**: enhance DNA repair, protein quality control, mitophagy
2. **Damage dilution**: periodic cell replacement (stem cell therapy)
3. **Metabolic slowing**: reduce metabolic rate (caloric restriction, mTOR inhibition)

## E187: Reversibility of Aging
**SCVC**: Epigenetic clock is partially reversible (Yamanaka factors). Physical basis:
- DNA methylation patterns ∝ (DNMT/TET enzyme kinetics)
- Histone modifications: acetylation ∝ (HAT/HDAC balance)
- Partial reprogramming (OSKM cyclical expression) can reset epigenetic age without losing cell identity

## E188: Complete Longevity Protocol
1. **Dormancy** (periodic metabolic suppression): ~10°C hypothermia → metabolic rate ~10% → damage accumulation ~10× slower
2. **Redundancy**: stem cell reserves for organ regeneration
3. **Nano-maintenance**: molecular machines for protein aggregate clearance, lipofuscin removal, cross-link reversal

## E189: Neuronal Nano-Maintenance Frequency
Neurons are post-mitotic → accumulate damage over decades. SCVC estimate: nano-maintenance needed ~1× per month for synaptic protein turnover, ~1× per year for genomic maintenance.

## E190: Cryopreservation vs Dormancy
- **Cryopreservation** (<-130°C): complete metabolic arrest. Damage: ice crystal formation, cryoprotectant toxicity
- **Dormancy** (0-10°C): metabolic rate ~5-10%. Damage: slower but continuous. Advantage: reversible without toxicity

SCVC: Dormancy superior for living organisms. Cryopreservation only viable if ice damage can be eliminated.

## E191: Human Dormancy Quantitative Protocol
- Temperature: 10°C (safe, avoids cardiac arrhythmia)
- Duration: up to 14 days (longer requires nutritional support)
- Metabolic rate: ~10% of baseline
- "Aging" during dormancy: ~0.04 years per day of dormancy (10% × 365/1)
- Net effect: 14 days dormancy = ~0.5 years of "effective" aging → minimal cost
