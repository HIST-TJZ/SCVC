# E183: Cancer Prevention Lock

**SCVC**: Cancer prevention = intervene before the mutation time window (E169) accumulates enough drivers.
- Annual screening can detect pre-cancerous lesions
- Immune surveillance enhancement: vaccine against common neoantigens
- DNA repair enhancement: upregulate repair enzymes pharmacologically

Physical basis: E168-E171 provide the intervention framework. Prevention is mathematically superior to treatment (catch before escape velocity).

# E185: Symbiotic Microbiome Evolutionary Lock

**SCVC**: Host-microbiome co-evolution creates mutual dependence. Physical basis:
- Nutrient cross-feeding (metabolic byproduct exchange)
- Immune tolerance (MHC-I recognition of commensal peptides)
- Niche exclusion (preventing pathogen colonization)

Lock strength ∝ (number of interdependent metabolic pathways) × (evolutionary time).
Cannot be "hacked" without disrupting the entire network.
