## §1 The Physical Ceiling of Bacterial Resistance

### 1.1 Why Bacteria Can't Become "Immune to Everything"

```
Bacterial resistance mechanisms all require PROTEINS:
  → Efflux pumps: ~3-5 genes, ~500-1,000 aa each → ~2,000-5,000 ATP/s to maintain
  → Target modification enzymes: ~2-4 genes → ~1,000-3,000 ATP/s
  → Antibiotic-degrading enzymes (β-lactamases): ~1-3 genes → ~1,000-2,000 ATP/s
  → Reduced permeability (porin loss): 1-2 genes → zero ATP cost (loss of function)
  → Target bypass (alternative metabolic pathway): ~5-10 genes → ~5,000-10,000 ATP/s

Each resistance gene:
  → Requires continuous protein synthesis (~1,600 ATP/protein × turnover rate)
  → Occupies ribosomal capacity (~10-20% of a ribosome's time per highly-expressed gene)
  → Competes with essential genes for cellular resources
```

### 1.2 The ATP Budget Crisis

```
A typical bacterium's ATP budget: ~10⁷ ATP/s
  → Basal metabolism: ~5×10⁶ ATP/s (50%)
  → Growth/division: ~3×10⁶ ATP/s (30%)
  → Remaining for "optional" functions (including resistance): ~2×10⁶ ATP/s (20%)

Cost per resistance gene (high expression): ~3×10⁵ ATP/s
  → 1 gene: 3% of optional budget → negligible
  → 5 genes: 15% → noticeable growth slowdown
  → 10 genes: 30% → significant fitness cost
  → 15 genes: 45% → severe growth disadvantage
  → 20 genes: 60% → cannot compete with wild-type → eliminated from population

→ The "resistance ceiling" is ~15 simultaneously maintained resistance genes
→ Beyond this: the bacterium spends more energy defending than growing
→ Natural selection REMOVES excess resistance genes when antibiotics are absent
```

## §2 The Clinical Implication — Why "15 Antibiotics" Works

### 2.1 E158's 15-Combo Strategy

```
SCVC prediction (E158):
  If you administer 15 different antibiotics simultaneously,
  each targeting a different resistance mechanism →
  
  The bacterium needs ~15 resistance genes to survive ALL of them.
  But 15 genes = ATP bankruptcy → cannot grow → cannot cause infection.
  
  → "15-combo" = forcing the bacterium past its physical-resource ceiling
  → Even if it HAS all 15 resistance genes, it can't USE them all simultaneously
  → The bacterium's own ATP budget becomes the drug

This is the SAME logic as E172 (cancer 4-combo):
  → Cancer cell ATP budget ~10⁹ ATP/s → 4 simultaneous pressures → 55-85% deficit
  → Bacterium ATP budget ~10⁷ ATP/s → 15 simultaneous pressures → >100% deficit
  → Same principle: "Weak × Many = Strong" — but "many" is ~15 for bacteria
```

### 2.2 Why This Isn't Used

```
Pharma reality:
  → Testing 15-drug combinations: regulatory nightmare
  → Each drug made by different company → no one can patent the combo
  → Low profit per drug (all generic) → no commercial incentive
  → "The cure for antibiotic resistance exists in theory.
     It doesn't exist in practice because no one can make money from it."
  
  → This is an INSTITUTIONAL failure, not a scientific one
  → ABC would eliminate the profit barrier → public health priority
```
