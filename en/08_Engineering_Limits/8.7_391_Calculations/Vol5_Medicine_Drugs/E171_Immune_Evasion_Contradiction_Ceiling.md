SCVC Medical Engineering  E171  Immune Evasion Contradiction Ceiling
====================================================================

【Core physical constraint】: Cancer cells face an inescapable contradiction — to evade T cells they must reduce MHC-I, but this triggers NK cells. Both recognition systems are locked by SCVC molecular parameters.

---

1. The MHC-I / NK Dual Lock
==============================================================

1.1 The Missing-Self Hypothesis
--------------------------------------------------------------
    ⚫ T-cell recognition: requires MHC-I + peptide → TCR binding (H-bond ~0.3 eV/bond)
    ⚫ NK-cell recognition: inhibited by MHC-I → if MHC-I is absent → kill

    Cancer cell's dilemma:
      Reduce MHC-I → evade T cells ✓ → but activate NK cells ✗
      Maintain MHC-I → be killed by T cells ✗ → but inhibit NK cells ✓

    ⚫ This is the SCVC "immune evasion contradiction" —
      cancer cannot simultaneously evade both arms of the immune system.
      The binding energies of both recognition systems (TCR–pMHC, KIR–MHC-I)
      are locked by α; there is no "third option."

1.2 The Physical Window Cancer Exploits
--------------------------------------------------------------
    Partial MHC-I downregulation:
    · Reduce to ~10–30% of normal → T cells cannot recognize (below activation threshold)
    · But still enough to inhibit NK cells (NK inhibition threshold is lower)

    SCVC constraint:
    · TCR activation threshold: ~10 MHC-peptide complexes (from H-bond cooperativity)
    · KIR inhibition threshold: ~1 MHC-I molecule (single ligand sufficient)
    · → Cancer's exploitable window: MHC-I at 1–10% of normal level
    · → This "immune escape niche" is a direct consequence of SCVC-locked binding affinities


2. Engineering Implications
==============================================================

2.1 Breaking the Contradiction
--------------------------------------------------------------
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │ Strategies to force cancer back into the immune crossfire:│
    │                                                        │
    │ 1. Upregulate MHC-I on cancer cells                     │
    │    · IFN-γ, epigenetic modifiers                        │
    │    · Forces cancer → T-cell visible → killed            │
    │                                                        │
    │ 2. Activate NK cells even at partial MHC-I               │
    │    · NK checkpoint blockade (anti-KIR, anti-NKG2A)       │
    │    · Lowers the NK inhibition threshold                  │
    │    · → Cancer can no longer "coast" at partial MHC-I    │
    │                                                        │
    │ 3. Bispecific engagers                                   │
    │    · Force T-cell–cancer synapse regardless of MHC-I    │
    │    · BiTEs, ImmTACs                                      │
    │                                                        │
    │ 4. CAR-T cells                                          │
    │    · MHC-I-independent recognition                       │
    │    · → Sidestep the contradiction entirely              │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

2.2 SCVC Verdict
--------------------------------------------------------------
    The immune evasion contradiction is a physical constraint favoring the host.
    Cancer's escape window is narrow (~1–10% MHC-I) and can be closed by:
    (a) Forcing MHC-I back up
    (b) Lowering NK inhibition threshold
    (c) Using MHC-I-independent recognition (CAR-T, BiTEs)

    ⚫ The contradiction cannot be "evolved away" — it is a direct consequence of
      two independently evolved immune recognition systems, both physically
      locked by SCVC molecular parameters.
