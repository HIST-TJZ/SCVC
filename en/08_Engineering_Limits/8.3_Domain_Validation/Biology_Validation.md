## Counterexample: Insect Tracheal System

Insects lack lungs — they deliver oxygen directly to tissues via tracheae. This limits their maximum body size (Carboniferous giant dragonfly Meganeura had a wingspan of ~70 cm; today''s insects are far smaller).

Why didn''t evolution "solve" the insect respiratory limitation? Because lungs require a circulatory system (heart + blood vessels + hemoglobin), and the complexity of a circulatory system requires specific body architecture — an architecture not selected within the insect developmental program. **Evolution is not omnipotent — it is constrained by existing developmental pathways.** But this is not an SCVC wall — it is an evolutionary-history wall. The distinction itself is important: some walls are physical ($\alpha$-locked), some are historical (developmental-pathway-locked).
