# 8.4 SCVC Methodology in Chemistry

**Version**: V3.0 | **Date**: 2026-07-24

SCVC contribution to chemistry is not replacing quantum chemistry — it is explaining **why** quantum chemistry parameters have the values they do.

### Three Layers of SCVC Chemistry

1. **Scale layer**: $\alpha$ sets Coulomb force strength → all bond energies $\propto \alpha^2$. $m_e$ sets length scale → all bond lengths $\propto a_0 = \hbar/(\alpha m_e c)$.
2. **Mechanism layer**: Vortex ring Ampère force replaces exchange integral. Pauli exclusion = topological repulsion of same-circulation vortex rings.
3. **Prediction layer**: Slater screening constants from Coulomb integrals (σ=0.3477, −0.67% deviation). Electronegativity from Z_eff geometry. Madelung lattice energies (3.1% average deviation).

### What SCVC Does NOT Replace
Quantum chemistry still needed for: exact bond lengths, vibrational spectra, reaction pathways. SCVC provides the parameter origin; QM provides the precision.

# 8.6 Self-Check Guide

For each engineering limit claim, verify:

1. **Input constants**: Are they from SCVC geometry or from measurement?
2. **Equations**: Are they standard textbook equations or SCVC-invented?
3. **Numerical check**: Can you reproduce the number with a calculator?
4. **Experimental comparison**: Is the experimental value within the claimed deviation?
5. **Honesty label**: Is the 🟢/🟡/🔴 label appropriate?

### Red Flags (SCVC Overclaims)
- If SCVC claims to replace a standard equation without showing the derivation
- If a ceiling is claimed as "absolute" but depends on engineering assumptions
- If the deviation is suspiciously perfect (0.00% for multiple unrelated quantities)

### Green Flags (SCVC Works)
- Standard textbook formula + SCVC constant → number matches experiment within stated uncertainty
- Multiple independent paths give the same constant (e.g., α from DH sum AND from Geiger-Nuttall slope)
- Falsifiable predictions that can be tested within 5-10 years
