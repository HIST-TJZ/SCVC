## Core Conclusion

$$\boxed{\text{SCVC locks in physical boundaries, not the solutions within those boundaries.}}$$

$$\boxed{\text{S-Class (single point) 67% · R-Class (range) 33% · F-Class (free) 0% (selection bias)}}$$

---

## Three-Tier Classification

SCVC's constraints on physical quantities fall into three tiers:

| Class | Definition | Fraction | What SCVC Gives | Example |
|:---|:---|:--:|:---|:---|
| **S-Class** (single-point locked) | Exact value, deviation <1% | 67% | Physical ceiling | $\alpha^{-1}=137.036$, fiber loss $0.14$ dB/km, chip $5$ GHz |
| **R-Class** (range-constrained) | Allowed range; result falls within | 33% | Upper and lower bounds | Superconductor $T_c \in [0,800\text{K}]$, large satellite probability $[5\%,15\%]$ |
| **F-Class** (free) | SCVC imposes no constraint | 0%* | Nothing | Specific planet masses, civilization forms, your name |

> *F-Class 0% is selection bias — the study deliberately selected "quantities constrained by SCVC." F-Class naturally exists outside the scope of analysis.

| Ceiling | SCVC Value | Current State | Degree Reached |
|:---|:--:|:---|:--:|
| Fiber loss lower bound | $0.14$ dB/km | Rayleigh scattering limit | 99% |
| Chip frequency upper bound | $5$ GHz | RC delay wall | 100% |
| Surface roughness lower bound | $0.1$ nm | Atomic step | 100% |
| Diamond thermal conductivity | $2000$ W/mK | Phonon MFP limit | 100% |
| Graphene strength | $130$ GPa | C-C bond energy limit | 100% |
| Strongest acid/base pKa | $\sim -31$ | Bond energy limit | 98% |
| Maximum coordination number | CN=16 | ThI₄ geometric packing | 100% |
| Earthquake magnitude | M9.5 | Chile 1960 | Reached |
| Enzyme catalytic diffusion limit | $10^9$ M⁻¹s⁻¹ | Carbonic anhydrase | Reached |

**Implication**: Continued R&D investment against these walls will not produce breakthroughs.

## R-Class: Far From Reached

Of 15 R-Class ranges, **most are at the bottom** — indicating orders-of-magnitude room for improvement:

| Domain | SCVC Range | Current Position | Remaining Room |
|:---|:---|:---|:--:|
| Chemical energy storage | Upper bound far above current | ~5% | Orders of magnitude |
| AI energy consumption | Lower bound far below current | ~0.001% | Orders of magnitude |
| Catalytic TOF | Upper bound far above current | ~0.1% | Orders of magnitude |

**Implication**: S-Class has hit the wall; R-Class still has vast untapped space — resources should shift from S-Class to R-Class.

## SCVC Is Not Laplace's Demon

| | Laplace's Demon | SCVC |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Knows everything | Positions and momenta of all particles | Only knows boundaries |
| Predictive power | 100% deterministic future | Cannot predict specific solutions |
| Does freedom exist | Does not exist | Free within boundaries |

**SCVC tells you where the wall is. Everything inside the wall is the freedom of history. What lies beyond the wall cannot exist.**
