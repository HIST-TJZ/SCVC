## KK Reduction: 7D → 4D

Standard Kaluza-Klein reduction of the 7D action on $M_{vac} \times CP^2$:

- Internal space isometry group → 4D gauge group
- Internal space volume → gauge coupling normalization
- Internal space curvature → cosmological constant and scalar potential
