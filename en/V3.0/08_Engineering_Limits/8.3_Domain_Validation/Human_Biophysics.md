## SCVC Derivation Chain

### Speech 39 bits/s

ATP hydrolysis $\Delta G=0.55$ eV follows from $\alpha$. The full chain: $\alpha$ → ATP → myosin → tongue speed → acoustic space F1-F2 → phoneme/gesture trade-off → $\tau_m\approx 20$ ms locks syllable rate.

Simultaneously, the auditory side: $\alpha$ → protein spring $k\propto\alpha^2$ → prestin protein conformation $5$-$10\ k_BT$ (body temperature 310K from $k_B$) → frequency resolution $\Delta f/f\approx 0.2\%$ → distinguishable phonemes 48-80.

**39 bits/s is a cortical $\tau_m$-locked ceiling, not a peripheral ceiling.** Both the auditory and articulatory organs can run faster — the brain cannot keep up.

### Lifespan ~120-130 years

Three irreducible sources of damage:

**Telomeres**: 50-100 bp lost per division. Telomerase = cancer. ATP cost locks the feasibility of whole-body telomerase.

**AGE crosslinks**: C-C crosslink bond 3.6 eV $\gg k_BT$ 0.026 eV. Body temperature cannot break them. Collagen replacement cost = 30-50% of daily ATP budget.

**mtDNA mutations**: Repair enzyme discrimination of oxidized bases $\Delta\Delta G\sim 0.1$-$0.3$ eV $\approx k_BT$ → leakage rate ~1% cannot be further reduced.

Three walls, take the minimum: ~120-130 years.

### Decision-making ~5/s

Cortical $\tau_m\approx 20$ ms → single decision requires 10-15 cycles (perception→integration→choice→execution) → ~5 decisions/s.

### Muscle ~200 W/kg

Myosin ATPase $k_{\text{cat}}$ activation barrier ~0.45 eV is determined by ATP hydrolysis free energy 0.55 eV (from $\alpha$). 200 W/kg is the absolute ceiling of myosin dynamics.

- 122 years is not "medicine not good enough" — body temperature at 310K cannot break 3.6 eV crosslink bonds. Not "can still be extended" — it is "thermodynamically forbidden."
- 5 decisions/s is not an attentional limit — the cortical membrane time constant is incompressible.
- 200 W/kg is not insufficient training — it is the physical limit of myosin. Bolt was merely the closest to the wall.

**AI is not subject to most of these limits. This is the $\alpha$-locked physical asymmetry between humans and AI.**
