## §2 The Secret in the Distribution — Why the Body Dominates

### 2.1 Brain Information Is "Fast," Body Is "Slow"

```
Brain:
  → Information rewritten at 100 bps (E82)
  → Synaptic weights drift daily
  → "You" today is different from "you" yesterday (but continuous)

Body:
  → Immune memory: written over decades, highly stable
  → Epigenetic marks: some are lifelong (early-life stress)
  → Scars: permanent (collagen does not remodel)
  → "You" at age 40 still carry cellular marks from age 5

→ The brain writes the daily "you"; the body writes the lifetime "you."
```

### 2.2 Body Provides Identity Continuity

```
If your brain were transplanted into a new body:
  → The immune system is new → you are allergic to things you were not allergic to
  → The microbiome is new → your food cravings and emotions change
  → No scars → you lose all physical history
  → Epigenetics are new → your stress responses change
  → Fascia tension patterns are new → your posture and movement habits change
  → Your brain notices all of this → "I am not me anymore"

→ Brain transplant = new person, not "you" in a new body.
```

```

  → Equivalent to growing a completely identical body from scratch
  → Requires replaying your entire life to accumulate equivalent immune/microbiome/epigenetic history

  → Forbidden
```
