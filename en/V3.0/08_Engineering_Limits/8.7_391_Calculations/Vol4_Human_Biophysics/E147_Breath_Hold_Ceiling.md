--------------------------------------------------------------------
§1. SCVC Physical Chain of Human Breath-Holding
--------------------------------------------------------------------

【O₂ Reserve — Three Warehouses】

  After pure-O₂ pre-breathing:
    Lungs (VC~4.8L×100%O₂): 4.8 L O₂
    Blood (Hb + dissolved): 1.1 L O₂
    Muscle (myoglobin): 0.06 L O₂
    **Total: ~6.0 L O₂**

【O₂ Consumption — Metabolic Floor】
  Resting metabolism: ~250 mL O₂/min → pure O₂ **available ~24 minutes**
  Dive reflex lowers metabolic rate ~20–30% in humans → O₂ available ~30–35 min

【CO₂ Drive — Chemical Timer for Mandatory Inspiration】
  Normal PaCO₂: 40 mmHg, pH 7.40
  Breath-hold breakpoint: PaCO₂ 70–80 mmHg (trained), 80–100 mmHg (elite + pure O₂)
  CO₂ production: ~200 mL/min → **CO₂ breakpoint: ~22 minutes (resting, pure O₂)**

  ▸ **O₂ and CO₂ simultaneously hit the wall at ~24 minutes!** — not coincidence, but the balance point of physiological design

【Where the Three Limits Intersect】
  Pure O₂ + dive reflex: theoretical ceiling **~30–35 minutes**
  (Human dive reflex far weaker than marine mammals)

§3. SCVC Ceiling

  **Absolute maximum for mammals: ~200 minutes**

  Three incompressible O₂ consumptions:
    1. Brain metabolism: ~20% resting metabolism → cannot be reduced → continuous O₂ required
    2. Cardiac metabolism: myocardium's own O₂ consumption
    3. Thermoregulation: heat loss in cold water → must burn O₂ for warmth

  Even with infinite O₂ reserves:
    CO₂ accumulates to toxic levels in ~3 hours (>120 mmHg → pH<6.9 → brainstem depression)
    Lactate accumulates to >20 mM in ~2–3 hours
    Both intersect at ~200 minutes → **marine mammal SCVC hard ceiling**

  ▸ Cetacean 2-hour dives = already touching the SCVC-permitted ceiling
  ▸ Human pure-O₂ 24 minutes = at ~80% of human SCVC ceiling
