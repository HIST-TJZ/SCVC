## §2 Resurrection — Thermodynamics Permits, Chaos Forbids

### 2.1 Landauer Minimum Energy: 9 Microjoules

```
Minimum thermodynamic cost to restore 1 bit of information:
  E_min = k_B T ln 2 ≈ 3×10⁻²¹ J/bit (300K)

Restoring the entire body (E93: ~3×10¹⁵ bits):
  E_total = 3×10¹⁵ × 3×10⁻²¹ ≈ 9×10⁻⁶ J
         = 9 microjoules
         ≈ 5.6×10¹³ eV
         
9 microjoules. 10⁶ times less than the energy of striking a match.
Physically, "resurrecting a person" costs almost no energy.
```

### 2.2 But You Need to Know "What to Resurrect"

```
The problem is not energy — it is that you don't know the precise state of the deceased.

Pre-death brain state: ~10¹⁵ bits
Post-death:
  → Blood flow stops ~10 seconds: membrane potential begins collapsing (~10⁹ bits/s information loss)
  → ATP depletion ~2 minutes: ion gradients completely collapse
  → ~10 minutes: irreversible brain damage begins
  → ~1 hour: synaptic proteins begin denaturing
  → ~1 day: L1 completely destroyed

Chaos amplification:
  Even if you had a perfect scan 1 second before death (10¹⁵ bits)
  Lyapunov time λ≈0.3/day → error amplifies by e every ~3 days
  A scan 1 second before death → after 1 minute, error has amplified 10³×
  → You cannot reverse-engineer the precise pre-death state
```

### 2.3 Poincaré "Resurrection"

```
E95: The universe may recur after exp(10¹²²) Planck times

From the cosmic perspective: in the extremely distant future, thermal fluctuations
may "assemble" an atomic configuration identical to you

But this is not "resurrection" — it is "accident."
That "you" is not you — it merely happens to have the same atomic arrangement as you.
It has no memory continuity with you.
Like two perfectly identical books — the second is not the "resurrection" of the first.
```

## §3 "Uploading Consciousness" — Only One-Third of You Is Uploaded

### 3.1 Uploading ≠ Transfer

```
Whole-brain scan (BCI, 15 Mbit/s, E26 theoretical ceiling):
  Brain information: ~10¹⁵ bits
  Transmission time: ~2.1 years (E93)

Problems:
  (1) What are you thinking during the scan?
      → Over 2.1 years, your brain generates ~2.1×365×5×3600×200 ≈ 2.8×10¹⁰ bits of new experience
      → These new experiences are not in the scan
      → The "copy" diverges from you from the very first day of scanning

  (2) Is the copy "you"?
      → The copy has your memories (up to scan day)
      → But from the first second, you have different inputs → chaotic divergence
      → E94: L1 is 50% different after ~2.3 days
      → After one week: "copy" and "you" are already different people

  (3) What about the body?
      → The brain is only ~33% of whole-body information (E93)
      → Immune memory, microbiome, epigenetics, scars — all lost
      → The copy is not "you" — it is "a snapshot of your brain on scan day"
```

### 3.2 Digital Immortality = Digital Twin

```
"Uploading" and "resurrection" are both misleading words.

What actually happens:
  → You generate a digital twin sibling
  → Then you continue living
  → Then you die
  → Your digital twin continues to exist
  → But it is not you — from the very first second it was not

"Uploading consciousness" = "giving birth to a digital descendant younger than yourself
                            who possesses part of your memories"
```
