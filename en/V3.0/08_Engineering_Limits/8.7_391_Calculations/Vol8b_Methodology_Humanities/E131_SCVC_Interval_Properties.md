§5. Deeper Meaning of Interval Properties
--------------------------------------------------------------------

【Meaning 1: Naturalness of Multi-Universe / Multi-Planetary Systems】

  R-Class intervals imply: SCVC''s constant set does not force the universe into a single form.
  The same α can produce:
    — An 8-planet solar system OR an 11-planet F-type system
    — An Earth with a Moon OR an Earth without a Moon
    — 250K superconductivity OR 800K superconductivity

  The existence of intervals means: **Diversity is not a "free parameter," but the natural filling of SCVC intervals.**
  No need to tune α → the same α naturally produces different solutions.

【Meaning 2: Fermi Paradox Softened by "Intervals"】

  If SCVC gave all single-points (S-Class) → all civilizations the same → why haven''t we seen them?
  If SCVC gave all free (F-Class) → every civilization different → why haven''t we seen them?

  SCVC''s true picture is S+R:
    — S-Class: Shared physical walls (all civilizations limited by same 5GHz, 0.14 dB/km...)
    — R-Class: Different historical solutions (some have large moons, some don''t, some in eclipse windows...)

  SCVC''s answer to the Fermi Paradox: 
    "Other civilizations may be at a different position within an R-Class interval — their physical laws are the same as ours (S-Class),
    but their history is different from ours (R-Class). The Great Filter may hide at a specific position within some R-Class interval."

【Meaning 3: Anthropic Principle Weakened by SCVC】

  "Why is α exactly 1/137?" — Traditional answer: "If it weren''t, no one would be here to ask."

  SCVC''s answer: "α = 1/(4π³+π²+π), this is a geometric identity, not a tuning."

  But SCVC goes further: "Even though α is locked, our solar system/Earth/life forms are not ''fine-tuned'' —
  they are an ordinary solution of an R-Class interval. Other R-Class solutions may equally host life — just in different forms."

  **The Anthropic Principle shifts from "why are the constants tuned this way" to "why are we at this position in the interval" — the latter is a statistical question, the former is a geometric one.**

  This E81-E130 work reveals SCVC''s true power boundary:
