# SCVC Engineering Limit E15: Computing Physical Ceilings

**SCVC Constants**: Landauer limit 2.85×10⁻²¹ J/bit, Margolus-Levitin bound, Bremermann limit

Three fundamental computing limits: Landauer (energy per irreversible bit), Margolus-Levitin (operations per second per joule), Bremermann (maximum computation rate per mass). SCVC: 1 kg of "perfect computer" could perform ~10⁵⁰ operations/second. Current supercomputer: ~10¹⁸ ops/s. "Computing has 10³²× room to improve. We are barely getting started."
