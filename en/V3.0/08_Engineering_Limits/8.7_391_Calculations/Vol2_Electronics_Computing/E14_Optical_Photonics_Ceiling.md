# SCVC Engineering Limit E14: Optical Photonics Ceiling

**SCVC Constants**: Diffraction limit (λ/2NA), photon energy hν, nonlinear susceptibility χ², χ³

Optical resolution: Abbe limit d = λ/(2NA). Super-resolution (STED, PALM) bypasses this via molecular state switching but requires more photons. SCVC floor for optical switching energy: ~hν ~1 eV per bit. Current: ~10³-10⁶ photons/bit. "Light is fast but energetic. One photon carries ~1 eV. You cannot switch with less. That's the quantum of optics."
