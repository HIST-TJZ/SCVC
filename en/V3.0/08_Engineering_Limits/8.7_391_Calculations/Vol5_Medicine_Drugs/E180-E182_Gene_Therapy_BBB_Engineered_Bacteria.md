# E180: Gene Therapy Delivery Upper Limit

**SCVC**: Delivery efficiency limited by:
1. Vector packaging capacity (AAV: ~4.7 kb, lentivirus: ~8 kb)
2. Nuclear entry: nuclear pore complex ~39 nm → cargo size limit ~25 nm
3. Immune response to vector capsid

Physical ceiling: ~10¹⁴ vector genomes can be delivered systemically. Tissue-specific promoters reduce off-target. SCVC sets the physical size constraints on delivery vehicles.

# E181: Blood-Brain Barrier Upper Limit

**SCVC**: BBB = tight junctions between brain endothelial cells. Permeability ∝ (molecular size)⁻¹ × (lipophilicity).
- Small lipophilic molecules (<400 Da): passive diffusion
- Large/hydrophilic: requires active transport or receptor-mediated transcytosis

Physical ceiling: no molecule >~600 Da can passively cross BBB. SCVC: junction protein H-bond network ∝ α² → barrier integrity fundamentally α-dependent.

# E182: Lock-Down Engineered Bacteria

**SCVC**: Engineered bacteria (e.g., engineered E. coli, Lactobacillus) for:
- Gut microbiome therapeutics
- In-situ drug production (e.g., butyrate, neurotransmitters)
- Cancer-targeting bacteria

Physical constraint: bacterial mutation rate limits genetic stability to ~10²-10³ generations before function degrades. SCVC: DNA polymerase fidelity → evolutionary stability window.
