# E193: Fungal Body Temperature Firewall

**SCVC**: Why mammals are resistant to systemic fungal infections:
- Fungal enzymes optimized for ambient temperature (~25°C)
- Mammalian body temperature (~37°C) → fungal enzyme kinetics impaired
- Thermal gap: ΔT ≈ 12°C → reaction rate reduction ~2-3× (Q₁₀ ≈ 2-3)

This is a physical firewall: fungi cannot easily evolve 37°C tolerance because it requires simultaneous enzyme changes across hundreds of proteins. SCVC: enzyme thermal stability ∝ (H-bond network energy) ∝ α² → changing temperature optimum requires global protein remodeling.

**Climate Change Threat**: Global warming → fungi evolve higher temperature tolerance → new fungal pathogens may cross the 37°C barrier → novel infectious disease threat.
