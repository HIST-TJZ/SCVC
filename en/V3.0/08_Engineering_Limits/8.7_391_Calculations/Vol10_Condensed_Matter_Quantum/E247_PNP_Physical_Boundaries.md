# PNP (Physics-Neutral-Point) Physical Boundaries

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟢 SCVC identifies genuine physical limits vs engineering limits

## Core
Distinguish between:
- **Physical limits**: Cannot be exceeded in this universe (e.g., speed of light)
- **Engineering limits**: Can be improved with better technology (e.g., battery energy density)

### Key Insight
Most "limits" in engineering are not fundamental physical limits — they are materials engineering limits. SCVC helps distinguish which are which by tracing each limit to its geometric origin in α.
