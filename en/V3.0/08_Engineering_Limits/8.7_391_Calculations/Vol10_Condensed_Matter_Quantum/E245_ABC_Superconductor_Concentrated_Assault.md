# ABC Superconductor Concentrated Assault

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 Strategic resource allocation plan

## Core Strategy
Under ABC society resource allocation, concentrated assault on room-temperature superconductor:

### Phase 1: Computational Screening (Year 1)
- DFT high-throughput screening of 10⁵ ternary hydrides
- SCVC θ_D formula as first-pass filter
- Target: Top 100 candidates for synthesis

### Phase 2: Parallel Synthesis (Years 2-3)
- 100 parallel diamond anvil cell stations
- Automated characterization
- Target: 10 materials with Tc>200K

### Phase 3: Metastable Quenching (Years 3-5)
- Focus on Be/B-based hydrides (highest quench probability per SCVC)
- Laser-heated diamond anvil cell + rapid decompression
- Target: 3 materials with Tc>150K at ambient pressure

### Phase 4: Industrial Scale-Up (Years 5-10)
- Large-volume press development
- Wire fabrication
- Target: Commercial room-temperature superconductor wire

### Resource Estimate
- Personnel: 500 researchers + 200 engineers
- Equipment: 100 DAC stations, 10 large-volume presses
- Computing: 10⁶ GPU-hours/year
- Total: ~$5B over 10 years

## Honesty: This is strategy, not physics. SCVC provides the scientific rationale for resource allocation priority.
