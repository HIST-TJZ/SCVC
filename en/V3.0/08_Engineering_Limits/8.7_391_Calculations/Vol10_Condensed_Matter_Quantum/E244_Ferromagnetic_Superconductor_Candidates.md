# Ferromagnetic Superconductor Candidates

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 Coexistence conditions from SCVC

## Core Problem
Superconductivity (anti-parallel vortex ring pairing) vs Ferromagnetism (parallel vortex ring alignment) are generally antagonistic.

### SCVC Coexistence Condition
Coexistence possible when:
1. Superconducting vortex rings and magnetic vortex rings inhabit different orbital subspaces
2. Or: magnetic order is of the Ising type (collinear) allowing superconducting pairs in orthogonal direction

Ferromagnetic superconductor candidates: layered systems where magnetic layer (vortex ring parallel alignment) and superconducting layer (anti-parallel pairing) are spatially separated → proximity-effect coexistence.
