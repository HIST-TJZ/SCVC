# Space-Based Superconducting Wireless Power Transmission

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 Engineering concept

## Core Concept
Room-temperature superconductors enable:
1. Lossless power transmission cables
2. Ultra-high-field magnets for compact fusion
3. Space-based solar power with superconducting transmission

### SCVC Physical Limits
Maximum transmission distance limited by:
- Cable mass (must be lifted to orbit)
- Thermal management (even superconductors need cooling below Tc)
- Cosmic ray damage to superconducting state

### SCVC Feasibility Assessment
| Application | Tc_needed | Feasibility | Timeline |
|:---|:--:|:---|:---|
| Terrestrial power grid | >250K | 🟡 Needs quenching success | 15-25 yr |
| Space solar power | >150K | 🟡 Space is cold (3K), easy to cool | 10-20 yr |
| Fusion magnets | >77K | 🟢 Even LN2-cooled helps enormously | 5-15 yr |
| Maglev transport | >250K | 🟡 Same as power grid | 20-30 yr |

## Honesty: Engineering concept dependent on room-temperature superconductor discovery first.
