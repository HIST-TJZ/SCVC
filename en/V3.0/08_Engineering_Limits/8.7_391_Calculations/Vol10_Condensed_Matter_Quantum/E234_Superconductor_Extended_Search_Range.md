# Superconductor Extended Search Range

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 Search heuristic

## Core
Expand superconductor search beyond traditional BCS electron-phonon mechanism.

### SCVC-Extended Search Categories

1. **Standard electron-phonon**: Hydrides, MgB₂-type → SCVC θ_D formula guides
2. **Ampère-direct pairing**: Two vortex rings directly couple without phonon mediation → possible in low-carrier-density systems
3. **Topological superconductors**: Vortex ring chirality → p-wave pairing → Majorana modes
4. **Excitonic superconductors**: Vortex ring-anti-ring pairs (analogous to exciton insulator mechanism)

### SCVC Prediction
The Ampère-direct mechanism may work in materials where electron vortex ring density is low enough that Coulomb repulsion is screened but Ampère attraction persists → extremely low carrier density superconductors.
