# Exciton Condensation and Quantum Spin Liquid

**Date**: 2026-07-25 | **Status**: 🟡 SCVC exciton = vortex ring-anti-ring pair

## Core
Exciton = bound electron-hole pair. In SCVC: vortex ring (electron) + anti-vortex ring (hole) Coulomb-attracted pair.

### Exciton Condensation Condition
```
n_exc · a_B*³ ∼ 1 (Mott criterion)
T_c ∼ (n_exc^(2/3)/m_exc) (BEC limit)
```

### Quantum Spin Liquid SCVC Picture
QSL = frustrated magnet where vortex ring orientations never freeze into ordered pattern.
→ Persistent vortex ring orientation fluctuation → fractionalized excitations (spinons).
