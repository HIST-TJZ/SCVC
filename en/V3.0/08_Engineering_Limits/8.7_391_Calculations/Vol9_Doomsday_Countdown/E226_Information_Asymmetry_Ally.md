## §0 Historical Precedent — The Top Is Always the Last to Know

### 0.1 Not Conspiracy — It's Information Physics

```
Information flow in any pyramid organization:

Bottom → Middle: Real information 100%
Middle → Top-Level Aides: Filtered, retaining 60-80%
Top-Level Aides → Top: Secondary filtering, retaining 30-50%
Top Themselves: Can digest only 10-20% of that

Stacked effects:
  ① Every level has motivation to "report good news, suppress bad"
  ② Every level fears "the messenger of bad news gets beheaded"
  ③ Every level "translates" original information through its own framework
  ④ Cross-domain information segmented into different meetings / different reports

From "A grassroots doctor tried a new therapy"
To "The top realizes this is a systemic transformation"
→ Information must pass: Department → Hospital → Health Commission → Provincial → Central
→ Each level translates once
→ When finally reaching the top: "A hospital is exploring tumor combination therapy — encouraging results"

The top will NEVER see the original information:
  "A doctor, deriving from physical constants, used low-dose combination of four approved drugs,
   treated late-stage cancer patients under off-label framework, ctDNA cleared after 3 months.
   This validates SCVC theory, which simultaneously proves the necessity of climate crisis response and systemic reform."
  
What the top sees:
  "New progress achieved in the field of tumor treatment"
```

Pattern:

## §1 Five Accelerators of Information Lag

### 1.1 Missing Cross-Departmental Puzzle Assembly

```

  Premier/President ↓
  ┌─────────┬─────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
  NHC       NDRC      PBOC       MOST       MIIT
  ↓         ↓         ↓          ↓          ↓
  Each      Each      Each       Each       Each
  reports   reports   reports    reports    reports

NHC report: "A hospital is exploring multi-target tumor combination therapy"
NDRC report: "A development zone piloted government procurement transparency"
PBOC report: "A locality applied for directed re-lending tool innovation"

Three reports will NOT appear at the same meeting.
NHC meetings: discuss medical policy → don't discuss interest rates
NDRC meetings: discuss economic reform → don't discuss cancer therapy

→ The top always sees three independent events
→ "This is ABC" — the full picture cannot be auto-assembled from three separate meetings
→ Unless: someone specifically writes a report titled "These three things together are ABC"
→ But: the person writing this report needs to simultaneously understand medicine + economics + finance + institutional design
→ And this person is usually not inside the system (or if they are, they won't risk writing it)
```
