## §2. Six Locked Physical Bolts

### 2.1 Bolt ①: Surface Coal Is Exhausted

`
18th century: England had coal outcrops — you could pick it up on a hillside.
         Britain's first wave of industrialization (1760-1840) cumulatively burned ~500 Mt of coal.
         All from mines <50m deep.

2024: All global surface coal and shallow coal is exhausted.
      Remaining coal mines are at 500-1,500m depth.

To mine coal at 500m depth, you need:
  ├─ Drill rig ──→ high-strength alloy steel ──→ blast furnace (>1400°C) ──→ coke ──→ coal [DEAD LOOP]
  ├─ Dewatering pump ─→ steam engine / electric motor ──→ iron + copper ──→ coal [DEAD LOOP]
  ├─ Ventilation system → large fans ──→ electricity [DEAD LOOP]
  └─ Transport system → rails + mine carts ──→ iron ──→ coal [DEAD LOOP]

Physical calculation:
  Digging one shaft 500m deep, 5m diameter:
  Rock to remove: ~24,500 tons
  Minimum lifting energy: ~0.1 TJ ≈ 3 tons of coal
  → You use the energy of 3 tons of coal to "fetch" coal
  → But you don't have those 3 tons of coal to drive the hoist
  
SCVC: Coal's C-C bond energy 3.6 eV
  → Combustion releases solar energy fixed by Carboniferous plants via photosynthesis
  → That energy was geologically buried and "stored" in chemical bonds
  → Extracting it requires energy → shallow coal had extremely high EROI (~80)
  → Deep coal requires large upfront energy investment → no "first push" available
`

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Low-grade (needs beneficiation) 20 ~1800°C          ❌ Needs industrial civ

SCVC: Fe-O bond energy ~4-5 eV
  → Reducing Fe₂O₃ requires breaking Fe-O bonds
  → Charcoal (pure carbon) combustion max temperature ~1400°C (thermodynamic limit)
  → Coke (coal distillation product) can reach ~2000°C
  → No coal = no coke = insufficient temperature = cannot process low-grade ores

`

  18th-century Industrial Revolution used all >60% high-grade ores — these veins are exhausted
  Restarters face all <30% low-grade ores
  Beneficiation requires: steel crushers + flotation reagents + electricity → all products of industrial civilization
`

### 2.3 Bolt ③: Shallow Oil Is Depleted

`
Evolution of oil extraction depth:

  1859: Drake Well (Pennsylvania) — 21 meters to oil. Pick + simple derrick.
  1901: Spindletop (Texas) — 350 meters. Steam rotary rig.
  1940: Typical onshore field — 1,000 meters. Diesel rotary rig.
  1970: North Sea — 3,000 meters. Offshore platform + directional drilling.
  2010: Deepwater / shale oil — 5,000+ meters. Horizontal drilling + hydraulic fracturing + 3D seismic.

How deep can restarters drill?
  Human/animal power:   <50m    (bamboo pipe + rope)
  Simple wooden rig:    <200m   (needs iron drill bit)
  Steam-powered rig:    <1,000m (needs boiler + iron pipe + coal)
  
  → But <200m depth range has zero commercial oil reservoirs remaining
  → Restarters cannot reach oil
  → No liquid fuel = cannot power transport, agricultural machinery, generators
  → "Find an old well and restart it" → old wells are plugged, casing corroded, reservoirs depressurized
  → Requires workover rigs + cementing + acidizing → all industrial-era technology
`
