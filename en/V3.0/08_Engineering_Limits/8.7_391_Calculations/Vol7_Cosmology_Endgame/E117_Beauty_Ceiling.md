φ in human aesthetics = inherited signal of "quality":
  φ-proportioned face → suggests no major environmental disruption during development (disruption→deviation from φ)
  "Beautiful" face = φ proportions → unconscious health detection

φ is not "a human-invented aesthetic standard."

and the human brain learned to detect this algorithm → perceives it as "beautiful."

`
Beauty = low information complexity + high predictability + safety signal

  Low information complexity: symmetry / φ ratio → fast neural processing → "ease"
  High predictability:   pattern / rhythm → prediction confirmed → dopamine reward
  Safety signal:     familiarity / harmony → no threat → amygdala silent

"Ugliness":
  High information complexity + low predictability + threat signal
  Asymmetry / chaos → slow neural processing → "effort"
