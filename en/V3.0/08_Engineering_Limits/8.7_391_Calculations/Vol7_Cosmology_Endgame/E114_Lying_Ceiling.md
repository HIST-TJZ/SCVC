## §1 The Original Equilibrium: Why Honesty Is Cheaper

`
E106 already proved: in a Dunbar-150 society, the information cost of deception is too high.

Deceiving N people:
  Requires sending different covert messages to each.
  Total information ≈ N × log₂(possible lies).
  
  Dunbar 150 people: 150 × log₂(100) ≈ 150 × 7 ≈ 1050 bits.
  Speech bandwidth: 39 bits/s → need ~27 seconds to tell all the lies.
  
  Truth: tell one person ~7 bits → 1 person can expose it in <0.2 seconds.

Original equilibrium: lie publishing speed < truth exposure speed.
Under this condition, honesty is a Nash equilibrium (E108: cooperation rate 80-85%).
`

## §2 How the Internet Broke the Equilibrium

| Parameter | Original Humans | Internet Era |
|:---|:--:|:--:|
| N (audience) | ~150 | ≤10⁹ |
| Publishing speed | 39 bits/s (one mouth) | ~∞ (bots/troll farms/batch) |
| Exposure speed | 1 person×1 sec | N people/sec (theoretically faster) |
| Memory | ~10 yr decay | ~∞ (permanent records) |
| Noise | 5-10% | ↑↑ (information overload) |

`
The internet''s contradiction:
  Exposure speed is theoretically faster (N people can expose simultaneously).
  But noise has simultaneously exploded → exposure signal drowned in noise.
  
  Publishing speed has gone from 39bits/s to infinity (machine generation).
  Lie publishing speed far exceeds human conscious reception speed (200bits/s).

Key bottleneck: human consciousness bandwidth ~200bits/s.
  No matter how much exposure information exists → you can only process 200bits/s.
  Fake news publishing speed >> 200bits/s → you can never finish reading all the lies.
  Lies update the next batch before you finish processing the previous one.
`

## §3 The Physical Ceiling of Fake News

`
Definition: Fake news = lies whose publishing speed > exposure speed.

Maximum sustainable fake news density:
  ρ_fake = (publishing speed − exposure speed) / consciousness bandwidth

  Publishing speed → approaches infinity (machine generation)
  Exposure speed → limited by human consciousness bandwidth 200bits/s
  Consciousness bandwidth → 200bits/s (total exposure bandwidth of all humans)

  Total human consciousness exposure bandwidth:
  8×10⁹ people × 200 bits/s ≈ 1.6×10¹² bits/s
  
  This seems large. But:
  Each person can only focus on limited topics.
  Fake news is distributed across 10⁶ topics.
  Effective exposure bandwidth per topic ≈ 1.6×10¹² / 10⁶ ≈ 1.6×10⁶ bits/s.
  
  Meanwhile, machine-generated fake news:
  Per topic > 10⁹ bits/s (batch generation)
  
  → Lie publishing / exposure > 10³
  → Fake news is PHYSICALLY impossible to "completely expose."
  → For every lie exposed, 1000 new lies are born.

This is the physical ceiling of fake news:
  Not "is anyone exposing it."
  It is "consciousness bandwidth cannot catch up to generation speed."
`

## §4 Can Humans Break Through with Cognitive Organs?

`
Human fake news defenses:
  Critical thinking → requires consciousness bandwidth (200bits/s) → insufficient
  Fact-checking → requires time → lies generate faster
  Education → improves signal-to-noise ratio → but noise is still growing

The bottleneck is always: 200 bits/s.
As long as consciousness bandwidth < fake news generation speed,
fake news will always outrun exposure.

The solution is not on the human side.
The human side is locked by τ_m.
The solution is on the AI side: AI exposes AI-generated fake news.
But E105: humans cannot supervise whether AI is truly exposing.
`

Primitive society: lie publishing < exposure speed → honesty is equilibrium.
Internet era: lie publishing >> exposure speed → fake news is ineradicable.

The physical ceiling of fake news = human consciousness bandwidth.
