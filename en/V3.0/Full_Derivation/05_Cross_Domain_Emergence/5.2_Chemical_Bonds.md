## Madelung Constant and Lattice Energy

The Madelung constant for ionic crystals is determined by lattice geometry. In the Born-Haber cycle:

$$\Delta H_f = \sum(\text{Ionization Energy} + \text{Electron Affinity}) - U_{\text{lattice}}$$

where $U_{\text{lattice}} = N_A \cdot M \cdot \frac{Z^+ Z^- e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 R_0}$. SCVC directly yields $\alpha$ and ionic radii → no fitting required.
