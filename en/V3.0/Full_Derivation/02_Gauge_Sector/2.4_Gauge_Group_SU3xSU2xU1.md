## $CP^2$ Geometry → $SU(3)$

$CP^2 = SU(3)/U(2)$ is a homogeneous Kähler manifold. Its isometry group is $SU(3)$. After KK reduction, this isometry group becomes the 4D gauge group $SU(3)_c$ — the QCD color group.

The stabilizer $U(2)$ corresponds to the subgroup that preserves the BEC vacuum direction. The broken generators ($8 \rightarrow 4$) become the longitudinal modes of the massive gauge bosons.

## $M_{vac}$ Geometry → $SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$

$$M_{vac} = (S^2 \times S^1)/\mathbb{Z}_2$$

- $\text{Isom}(S^2) = SO(3) \simeq SU(2)$ → $SU(2)_L$
- $\text{Isom}(S^1) = U(1)$ → $U(1)_Y$
