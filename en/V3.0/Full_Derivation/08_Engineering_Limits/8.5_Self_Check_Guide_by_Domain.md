## How to Use This Table

1. Find your field
2. Look at SCVC's ceiling value
3. Compare with historical data in your field — already reached or close?
4. Look at the "Verification Method" column — one experiment falsifies SCVC
5. Two outcomes: falsify → the wall is soft, keep hitting. Confirm → save the wall-hitting budget

## Verification Logic

These are not "SCVC says this is impossible, so don't try."

They are:

**If SCVC is correct** → these walls are immovable → continued investment in these directions wastes funding.

**If SCVC is wrong** → you can prove it with a single experiment in your own field → SCVC collapses.

Either outcome benefits you. You don't need to learn differential geometry to verify — you just need to do what you do best in your own field: **one precise measurement.**

If not one of 128 ceilings can be breached — that's not 128 coincidences. That's 128 photographs of the same wall.
